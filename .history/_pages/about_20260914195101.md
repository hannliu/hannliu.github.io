Hi! I’m Hannah Liu, an MSc student in Computer Science at the University of Toronto. My research interests are in natural language processing and computational linguistics.

A lot of my work comes from a simple question: how well do language technologies actually work across different languages and linguistic communities? I’m particularly interested in building datasets and evaluation methods that help us understand where current models perform well, where they struggle, and why.

Outside of research, I enjoy math, programming, jigsaw puzzles, Lego, and learning languages.
