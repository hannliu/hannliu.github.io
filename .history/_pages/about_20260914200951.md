Hi! I’m Hannah Liu, an MSc student in Computer Science at the University of Toronto. My research interests are in natural language processing and computational linguistics. Though born in Toronto, I grew up in Shandong, China, and later moved back to Canada for university. I completed my undergraduate studies at the University of Toronto in Computer Science and Mathematics, where I gradually became interested in NLP through research and coursework.

Outside of research, I enjoy math, jigsaw puzzles, Lego, and learning languages. I also like picking up new technical skills and exploring topics outside my main area whenever something catches my interest.
